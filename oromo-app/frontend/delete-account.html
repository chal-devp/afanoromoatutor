Delete your Oromo Tutor account and data

In the app

  1. Open Oromo Tutor and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and type DELETE to confirm.

This permanently deletes your account (email) and your saved learning progress.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email [your email] from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account and progress within [30] days.

What is deleted, what is kept

Deleted: your email, login and learning progress. Mistake reports you sent may be kept without your email for [X months] to improve answers. Data already sent to our AI providers is handled under their own policies. See our privacy policy.
